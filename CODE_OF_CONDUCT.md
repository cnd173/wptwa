# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome people regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint. Discuss technical decisions with evidence and avoid harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, or disruption.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate this standard. Serious or sensitive conduct concerns should be reported privately through the repository's Security reporting channel rather than a public issue.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately and fairly as practical.
